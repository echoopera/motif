What it offers

An instrument you play by moving

No timeline, no loops to arrange. You decide where the music goes by choosing where to go.

Draw a route

Drag from any source across the map and play it back over 30 seconds to 5 minutes. Turn on Loop and it repeats until you stop it.

Four living soundscapes

Verdant, Tideline, Umbra and Ember each have their own scale, tempo and mood. They keep evolving while you listen.

Bring your own audio

Import up to four recordings of your own. CONTINUUM estimates BPM and key, and you can correct both. Files stay on your device.

Handoffs that stay in tune

Tempo and tonic align between sources. Harmony guard softens clashes between major and minor, so moving between worlds sounds intended.

Spatial depth

Each source sits at its own place in the field. Distance and direction shape how it sounds. Put on headphones to hear it.

Processing array

Five effects on the combined sound: granular, delay, reverb, cutoff with LFO and Lo-Fi Media. Twenty-five presets, each with its own dry and wet mix.

How it works

Three gestures

01

Initialize

Tap INIT to wake the sound field. Four sources appear as wireframe globes, each with a live level gauge.

02

Draw

Drag from a source and trace a path. Tap a source, or its tile below the map, to fly straight there.

03

Play

Press play. Your path becomes a composition. Loop it, reshape it, hold a section, then save the journey or export it as a file.

Play it live

The route is never fixed

Once a journey is playing you can keep working on it with your fingers.

  • Loop the journey. One tap and the route repeats on the beat, with no gap at the join.
  • Reshape while it plays. Drag any point on the route and it bends smoothly around your finger. The music keeps going.
  • Hold to loop a section. Press and hold on the route and the playhead circles that stretch, a whole number of bars long.
  • Release with the pulsing dot. Tap the dot on the line to carry on, or pause to stop everything.

Built for the phone

Native in the hand

The instrument is designed around one thumb. Everything you play with sits in the lower half of the screen.

  • Bottom tab bar. Nav, Mix, FX, Sources and Journeys, one tap each.
  • Thumb-zone controls. Play, save, clear and new are within reach of one hand.
  • Full screen. Add to Home Screen and it runs without the browser bars.
  • Stays awake. The screen holds while a journey plays.
  • Fits any screen. It adapts to small phones, landscape, iPad and desktop.
  • Private. Your audio and journeys stay in your browser.

Questions

Before you play

Do I need headphones?

No, but they help. The spatial field places each source to the left or right and changes its brightness with distance, and that is clearest in headphones.

Can I use my own music?

Yes. Import up to four recordings, each up to 8 minutes. CONTINUUM estimates tempo and key, and you can correct them. Matching can transpose a recording but cannot turn a major piece into a minor one.

Where is my audio stored?

In your browser on your device. Journeys are saved there too, and you can export any journey as a file. Exported files hold the path and settings, not the audio.

Why do I have to tap INIT first?

Browsers only allow sound after a tap. One tap wakes the audio engine, and the screen stays ready from then on.

Which devices does it run on?

Current Safari and Chrome on iPhone, iPad and desktop. Audio imports need a browser with AudioWorklet support.
