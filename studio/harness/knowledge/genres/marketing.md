# Digital Marketing & Brand Surfaces — Domain Intelligence

## Surfaces
Landing pages, campaign microsites, product pages, pricing, docs-as-marketing, email templates, social assets, launch films (storyboards), app store listings, brand systems.

## Principles
- One page, one promise, one action. Above the fold: what it is, who it's for, why now, the button.
- Show the product; don't describe it. Real UI, real motion, real numbers.
- Typography carries the brand; imagery proves the claim; motion draws the eye to the CTA.
- Performance and accessibility are conversion features.
- Consistency across every touchpoint: same tokens as the product, same voice as the copy deck.

## Patterns
| Need | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Hero | Headline (benefit) + subhead (how) + CTA + product visual; secondary CTA optional |
| Proof | Logos, quotes with faces, metrics with context |
| Features | Alternating media/text, or bento grid for many small proofs |
| Pricing | 2–3 tiers, recommended highlighted, toggle monthly/annual, FAQ below |
| Footer | Complete site map, legal, language |
| Email | Single column, 600 wide, dark-mode safe, one CTA |
| App store | First screenshot = the promise; captions as headlines; preview video under 15 s |

## Brand system deliverables
Logo system (lockups, clear space, minimum sizes), colour (primary, secondary, semantic, tints), type (display, text, mono; scale), imagery direction, iconography grammar, motion principles, voice and tone, templates.

## Visual language
Whatever the brand says — but always with a grid, a scale, and restraint. Trend-aware, not trend-driven.

## HALT
Dark-pattern CTAs · Fake urgency · Claims without proof · Contrast failures on hero text · Auto-playing audio.
