# Direction Memo
1. Chosen direction: name, thesis, why the client chose it.
2. Five rules every lane obeys (each with what it sacrifices).
3. Five things we will not do.
4. Three references by name only, and the one thing to take from each.
5. Motion personality (three adjectives). Voice (three adjectives).
6. The proof screen: which screen must nail the direction first, and who owns it.
Record in DDM `direction` block.
