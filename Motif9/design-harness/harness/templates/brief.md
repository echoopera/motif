**Users** · persona / goal / context / pain — one block each
**Platforms** · ios, ipados, web-immersive, web-saas, web-editorial, web-internal, macos

**Constraints** · tech, brand, legal, timeline
**Acceptance** · AC-1 … how we'll know it worked
**Out of scope** · what we are deliberately not doing
