# Glossary
- **Style** — one of the 25 procedural animation recipes.
- **Look** — a style plus all its parameter values.
- **Mutate** — nudge unlocked values by the chosen strength.
- **Evolve** — breed six variations and keep one.
- **Lock** — protect a value from Mutate, Evolve and Randomize.
- **Loop length** — seconds per seamless cycle.
- **Tempo** — how many motion cycles fit in one loop (whole numbers keep the loop seamless).
- **Phase** — where in the cycle the loop starts.
- **Preset** — a Look saved as JSON for reuse or for Motif.
